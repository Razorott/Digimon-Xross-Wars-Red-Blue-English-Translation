# Digimon Story: Super Xros Wars English translation

- Target language: English. Translate text first; Japanese graphics follow.
- Human names: Taiki, Akari, Zenjiro, Kiriha, and Nene. The user explicitly chose
  original character names on 2026-10-24. Retain Xros Heart and Xros Loader.
- Keep the ROMs under `Original Roms/` untouched. Generate builds separately.
- Research Digimon names, attacks, and franchise terms when translating them.
  Prefer official Western releases and official English card/game material.
  Record source URLs and conflicts in the glossary. Do not silently invent an
  official localization where none has been verified.
- Preserve the Japanese source, stable string IDs, control bytes, and version
  context. Never use an unrelated fan patch as the translation authority.
- Label incomplete translations and builds accurately. Structural validation
  does not establish that the game boots or that text fits on screen.
- Story text currently advances at a fixed 12 pixels per character, ignoring
  proportional NFTR metrics. The build wraps story at 15 columns. Check emulator
  screenshots after changing fonts, line wrapping, or text-table packing.
- Keep ROMs, extracted original assets, and generated ROMs out of Git.
