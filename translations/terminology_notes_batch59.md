# Batch 59 terminology and continuity notes

Japanese game text is the primary source for this batch. Human names stay in their Japanese forms. Western Digimon spellings were checked against Bandai's English card material when an entry was available; game-specific places, devices, ranks, and titles remain clearly marked as working renderings.

## Game-specific wording

| Japanese | English used | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ロストスペース | Lost Space | Existing project term. |
| パスファインダーデヴァイス | Pathfinder Device | Working translation of the device name; no Western game localization verified. |
| リンケージ　パス　コネクト | Linkage: Path Connect! | Working rendering of the device's activation message. |
| クモノス西のカギ / クモノス東のカギ | Kumonos Ruins West Key / Kumonos Ruins East Key | Follows the established Kumonos Ruins map names and the earlier West Key label. |
| ファラオモン・ザ・グレイブ | Pharaohmon the Grave | Pharaohmon is the species name; “the Grave” is the character's title rendered from the Japanese. |
| ポイズンリーダー / プレデター | Poison Leader / the Predator | ROG role and Arukenimon's epithet; translated from the game text, not a separate Digimon name. |
| いじょうなイビル / もんだいなイビル | “This is bad! We're in trouble!” | The two short Japanese alarm lines are a wordplay-like exchange. The English keeps their warning function rather than forcing an unclear literal pun. |

The story segment follows the source sequence: the team returns Cutemon, learns that Nene has been directed toward Lost Space, finds Sparrowmon under Arukenimon's control, then travels toward Kumonos Ruins and Papyrus Desert. The game script is the source for these events; they are not added from an anime retelling. The 34 technique descriptions are translated from their Japanese effect text. “Pendulum Blade” is a direct rendering of the named attack within its description, not a claim of a verified *Dawn/Dusk* match.
